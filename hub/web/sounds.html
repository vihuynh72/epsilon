Epsilon Messages Sounds Set up

Sounds

Some people have their own sound for a thing. Teach Epsilon a few examples, then let it listen. It only ever says what a sound may mean, and you decide.

  1. Teach. Record the sound for two meanings, three times each.
  2. Listen. Press Listen now and make one of the sounds, or say a word.
  3. Answer. Tell it if it was right. It learns from every answer.

Listen

Press the button, then make the sound. Epsilon listens for two and a half seconds and stops. It never listens on its own.

How it is doing

Teach

Record the sound for each meaning about three times. Only a fingerprint of the sound is kept, on this device. The recording itself is not saved.

Teaching a gesture

Gestures are taught on the band page. Open it in Chrome on a laptop, press Connect, and choose the band. Then pick a word and let your child show their own movement a few times. Nothing needs installing.

Open the band page

Sounds and gestures end up in the same place. Each one becomes a word, and the word goes to Messages.
