Epsilon Messages Sounds

One gesture. One message home.

Epsilon lets a child who does not speak ask for water, a break, or help with a small wrist movement. The people who care for them get it as a plain message.

How it works

  1. 1

    The gesture

    The wearer makes a wrist movement they were taught. The band recognises it.

  2. 2

    The message

    Each gesture maps to a sentence the family wrote. Software picks the sentence. It never invents one.

  3. 3

    The check

    A weak or doubtful match is held back, so parents are not sent messages their child did not mean.

What this is, plainly

  • A hackathon prototype, built in a day.
  • Not yet tested with autistic users.
  • It does not read minds. The wearer’s gesture comes first.
  • Nothing is ever sent on the wearer’s behalf.
