Epsilon Messages Sounds Set up

One gesture. One message home.

Epsilon is a wristband for a child who does not speak. They move their wrist, and their parent gets a text.

  1. 1

    The child makes a gesture

    One they taught the band: water, food, parents, sleep or help.

  2. 2

    Epsilon checks it is sure

    A decision model (Gemini, an AI) picks: send a text, send an urgent voice message, or wait if the match is doubtful.

  3. 3

    The parent gets a message

    An iMessage. Urgent words also come as a voice message.

One message, start to finish

An example of what happens in about a second.

A child moves their wrist the way they taught it.

Gemini checks the match and decides in under a second. It is clear, so it goes through.

I want water, please.

Delivered to the parent’s phone by iMessage, through Photon

Later, the child makes the gesture for help.

I need help!

Emergency. ElevenLabs speaks it as a voice message

The parent hears it, sees when and how it was sent, and goes to them.

If the child speaks or makes their own sound instead, Deepgram writes it down and Gemini works out the word. Each sound a parent confirms is saved, so Epsilon learns that child’s sounds.

At the end of the day, Gemini 4 Hydrogen reads the messages and writes a short summary with one suggestion.

What it is built with

  • Vexo Open bandThe wristband. Its motion sensor reads the gesture.
  • GeminiDecides each message in under a second: send a text, send an emergency voice message, or hold it back. It gives its reason.
  • Gemini 4 HydrogenReads the day’s messages and writes a short summary with one suggestion.
  • Gemini, listeningHears a recording of unclear speech and suggests which word it most likely is. A person confirms.
  • DeepgramWrites down spoken words on the Sounds page.
  • ElevenLabsSpeaks the emergency message. It arrives as a voice message.
  • PhotonDelivers the texts and voice messages by iMessage.
